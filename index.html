About Rayven

Rayven E. Burato (He/Him)

Aspiring Graphic Artist & Visual Storyteller

Based In Calamba, Laguna • Worldwide Remote
Experience 2025–2026 Graphic Artist — CCC Dibujo Creatives

Hi, I’m Rayven, a Graphic Artist who loves bringing ideas to life through thoughtful visual design.

My creative journey started in university, where I regularly jumped at the chance to design campus organization materials, event pubmats, and promotional graphics. Since then, I’ve had the privilege of collaborating with a wonderful variety of projects from cutting-edge tech startups and indie beverage brands to creative companies and streetwear labels.

While my personal artistic style tends to lean toward a fun mix of modern neo-brutalism and friendly cartoon warmth, I always put the client first. I love adapting to whatever aesthetic a brand needs, and I'm always eager to learn new skills or tackle new challenges to make sure the final output fits your exact vision.

At the core of my work is a simple belief: Functional clarity shouldn't mean boring minimalism. When we mix vibrant colors, tactile borders, and custom character illustrations into your brand touchpoints, your audience doesn't just look they remember.

HOW WE COLLABORATE

A Seamless, 4-Step Creative Journey

No endless revisions in the dark. Here is how your project goes from brief to breakthrough:

1
PHASE 01

Discovery & Creative Brief

We discuss your project goals, target audience, aesthetic preferences, and reference materials to define a clear creative direction.

Milestone: Creative Brief & Moodboard
2
PHASE 02

Sketching & Concept Exploration

I explore multiple layout directions, rough character sketches, and typographic systems in Canva, Figma, or Ibis Paint for your initial review.

Milestone: 2–3 Concept Directions
3
PHASE 03

Refinement & Fine-Tuning

We fine-tune the winning design—sharpening tactile borders, balancing high-contrast color harmonies, and perfecting typography.

Milestone: Refined Client Proofs
4
PHASE 04

Final Delivery & Hand-off

Master production files packaged in high-res PNG, JPG, SVG, and PDF formats ready for immediate digital deployment or print.

Milestone: Production Asset Kit
